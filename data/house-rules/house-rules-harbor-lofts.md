# Harbor Lofts — House Rules

Welcome to Harbor Lofts, a 24-unit building near the river walk. These rules apply to every guest and every person you invite in. Reading them takes five minutes and saves you a phone call at midnight.

## Check-in and check-out

- Check-in opens at **4:00 PM**. Early check-in is possible only if the front desk confirms it in writing the same day; the unit may still be being cleaned.
- Check-out is at **11:00 AM**. Late check-out costs **$40 per hour** and must be requested before 8:00 PM the night before.
- Your door code arrives by SMS **two hours before check-in**. It stops working at check-out time.
- The building door and the elevator need the same code. Do not share it with delivery drivers; use the lobby locker instead.

## Guests and visitors

- The maximum occupancy printed on your booking is the legal maximum for the unit, including children.
- Visitors are welcome between **9:00 AM and 10:00 PM**. Anyone staying past 10:00 PM counts as a guest and must be registered at the front desk with an ID.
- Parties and events are not allowed in any unit or on the roof terrace.

## Noise

- Quiet hours are **10:00 PM to 8:00 AM** every day.
- Between quiet hours, music and TV must not be heard from the hallway.
- Two noise complaints in one stay end the stay without a refund.

## Parking

- The building has **8 parking spots** in the garage on level B1. A spot must be booked in advance and costs **$18 per night**; it is not included in the reservation.
- Vehicles taller than **2.0 m** do not fit in the garage.
- Street parking is metered from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM, Monday to Saturday, and free on Sundays.
- A car parked in the garage without a booking is towed at the owner's expense.

## Smoking and pets

- Smoking and vaping are not allowed anywhere in the building, including balconies. The cleaning fee for smoking is **$300**.
- Pets are not allowed, except registered service animals with documentation sent before arrival.

## Deposits and damages

- A **$250 security deposit** is held on your card at check-in and released within **7 days** after check-out if no damage is found.
- Report anything broken on the day you arrive using the link in your welcome message; anything reported later is assumed to have happened during your stay.
- Lost key cards cost **$25** each.

## Trash and recycling

- Trash goes down the chute on each floor. Glass and cardboard go to the blue bins on level B1, never in the chute.
- Do not leave bags in the hallway; the building has been fined for it.

## Amenities

- The gym on the ground floor is open **6:00 AM to 11:00 PM** with your door code.
- The roof terrace closes at **10:00 PM** and has no lighting after that.
- Wi-Fi: network **HarborLofts-Guest**, password in your welcome message. Do not plug your own router into the wall port.

## Emergencies

- For a building emergency call the front desk at the number in your welcome message; it is answered 24 hours a day.
- Fire exits are at both ends of every hallway. Do not use the elevator during a fire alarm.
