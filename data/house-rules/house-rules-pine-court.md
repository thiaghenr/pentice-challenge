# Pine Court Residences — Guest Guide and Rules

Pine Court is a family building with long-term residents. Our guests share the hallways, the pool and the parking lot with people who live here all year, so the rules are stricter than in a hotel.

## Arrival

Check-in is from **3:00 PM**. If you arrive before that, you can leave your bags with the concierge, who is on site from 7:00 AM to 11:00 PM. There is no self check-in: the concierge meets you, checks an ID for every adult on the reservation, and hands over two physical keys.

Check-out is at **10:00 AM**. Leave both keys on the kitchen counter and pull the door closed. A missing key costs **$60** and forces a lock change.

## Who can stay

Only the people named on the reservation may sleep in the unit. Guests under 18 must be accompanied by an adult on the reservation. Visitors may come until **9:00 PM** and must sign in at the concierge desk.

## Quiet

Quiet hours are **9:00 PM to 7:00 AM**. The floors are wood, so please no hard shoes indoors after 9:00 PM. Residents report noise directly to the concierge, and the first complaint is a written warning. The second ends the stay.

## Pool

The pool is open **8:00 AM to 8:00 PM**. Children under 12 must be with an adult. No glass near the pool. Towels are in the unit; pool towels are not provided. The pool is closed on Mondays for cleaning.

## Cars

Each unit includes **one free parking space**, numbered to match the unit, in the outdoor lot. A second car costs **$12 per night** and needs a permit from the concierge; without a permit it is ticketed by the city. Do not park in a resident's numbered space even for a minute. There is no garage, so height is not a problem, but trailers and campers are not allowed.

## Pets

Dogs up to **15 kg** are welcome for a **$30 per stay** fee, maximum one per unit, always on a leash in common areas and never left alone in the unit for more than four hours. Cats and other animals are not allowed.

## Smoking

Smoking is allowed only in the marked area behind the parking lot. Smoking in a unit, on a balcony or by the pool costs a **$250** cleaning fee and may end the stay.

## Money

We do not hold a deposit. Instead, damage found at check-out is charged to the card on file, with photos sent to you within **48 hours**. Normal wear is never charged.

Late check-out is not available Sunday to Thursday. On Friday and Saturday it can be booked until **1:00 PM** for **$50**, subject to the next arrival.

## Kitchen and laundry

The unit has a full kitchen. The shared laundry room on the ground floor takes the building app, not coins; the concierge sets it up for you at check-in. Laundry closes at **10:00 PM**.

## Waste

Sorting is mandatory in this city. Three bins are under the sink: general, organic, recyclables. Bags go to the shed by the parking lot, never to the hallway. Bulky items cannot be left anywhere on the property.

## Help

The concierge desk is your first call for anything during opening hours. After 11:00 PM, the emergency number in your welcome message reaches the building manager for urgent issues only: no water, no power, a lockout or a safety problem. Lockouts after 11:00 PM cost **$80**.
